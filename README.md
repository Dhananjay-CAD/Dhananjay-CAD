## About Me

I'm a mechanical design engineer who bridges the gap between concept and production. My work spans **medical devices, IoT hardware, robotics, consumer appliances, and industrial machinery** — always with an eye on manufacturability, cost reduction, and real-world performance.

At **Inventindia Innovations**, I led end-to-end NPD for 15+ client projects, from competitive benchmarking and FEA simulation through prototyping and tooling sign-off. Currently at **Innovix Pro**, I architect robotic mechanical systems with 95%+ motion precision and drive 20% BOM cost reductions through DFMA-optimised designs.

I hold an M.Tech from **Nirma University**, a Certified SOLIDWORKS Professional badge, and a Six Sigma Green Belt. I also hold a **patent** for a remotely controlled toggle switch.
